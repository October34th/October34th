## About

I work where **design**, **writing**, and **code** meet: privacy-first software, visual communication, and source-based research on Chinese history, language, and literature.

My projects usually begin with a practical problem or an overlooked body of material. I build tools that are useful, transparent, and easy to inspect, and I organize research so that it remains readable, verifiable, and open to discussion.

> The best interfaces, like the best sentences, are the ones you do not notice.
