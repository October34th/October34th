### ◆ About

I work in the seam between **design**, **writing**, and **code** — brand and
architectural visualization on one side, long-form research and essays on the
other, and a small pile of self-hosted tools holding the middle together.

- 🎨 Visual work: brand systems, poster design, photoreal architectural renders
- ✍️ Written work: long-form essays and research, mostly on culture and language
- 🔐 Technical work: privacy-first tooling, self-hosting, small utilities I actually use
